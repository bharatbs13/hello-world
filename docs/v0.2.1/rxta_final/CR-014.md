# CR-014 — Topology Profile Planner

## Target Version

v0.2.1

## Status

Draft

## Objective

Generate execution topology definitions for RXTA through metadata-driven topology planning and depth planning. The framework defines topology profiles, connector participation, infrastructure role participation, topology signatures, infrastructure signatures, and depth mappings so that execution planning can consume a consistent topology model without owning topology construction. The framework remains independent of execution, validation, reporting, domain selection, and solution selection.

## Scope

### Owns

- topology profile definitions
- topology planning
- depth planning
- connector participation planning
- connector inventory planning
- infrastructure role planning
- topology signature generation
- infrastructure signature generation
- d_mapping.json generation

- execution identity
- suite selection

- infrastructure selection
- domain selection
- solution selection
- deployment
- validation execution
- report aggregation
- diagnostic analysis
- test execution logic

- topology profile definitions
- depth profile definitions
- connector inventory
- infrastructure inventory
- catalog metadata

## Outputs

- topology plan
- d_mapping.json
- topology_signature
- infra_signature
- connector participation metadata
- infrastructure role metadata

## Requirements

### R1.

RXTA shall support topology profile definitions.

### R2.

RXTA shall support depth profile definitions.

### R3.

RXTA shall generate topology plans from topology profile definitions.

### R4.

RXTA shall generate depth plans from depth profile definitions.

### R5.

RXTA shall determine connector participation for each topology plan.

### R6.

RXTA shall determine infrastructure role participation for each topology plan.

### R7.

RXTA shall generate d_mapping.json for each topology and depth plan.

### R8.

d_mapping.json shall describe the logical depth mapping required by the topology plan.

### R9.

RXTA shall generate a topology_signature for each topology plan.

### R10.

topology_signature shall identify the logical topology shape.

### R11.

RXTA shall generate an infra_signature for each topology plan.

### R12.

infra_signature shall identify the infrastructure role participation shape.

### R13.

Topology signatures shall be deterministic for identical topology plans.

### R14.

Infrastructure signatures shall be deterministic for identical infrastructure role participation plans.

### R15.

Topology planning shall be independent of suite selection.

### R16.

Topology planning shall be independent of execution identity generation.

### R17.

Topology planning shall be independent of validation execution.

### R18.

Topology planning shall be independent of report aggregation.

### R19.

Topology profiles shall not encode suite membership.

### R20.

Topology profiles shall not encode execution result expectations.

### R21.

Topology profiles shall not encode validation pass or fail logic.

### R22.

Topology planning outputs shall be consumable by execution matrix planning.

### R23.

Topology planning outputs may be recorded in execution context as optional execution dimensions.

### R24.

topology_signature may participate in execution_id generation when present, as defined by CR-016.

### R25.

RXTA shall support topology profiles for current topology examples: single_db and source_target.

### R26.

Topology profiles may be associated with domain metadata through catalog metadata.

### R27.

Topology profiles may be associated with solution metadata through catalog metadata.

### R28.

CR-014 shall not own domain selection.

### R29.

CR-014 shall not own solution selection.

## Architecture Notes

### Current Topology Examples

| Topology | Purpose |
|----------|----------|
| single_db | Single logical participant |
| source_target | Source and target logical participants |

### Topology Planning Model

```text
topology profile
        ↓
connector participation
        ↓
infrastructure role participation
        ↓
topology plan
        ↓
topology_signature
        ↓
infra_signature
```

### Depth Planning Model

```text
depth profile
        ↓
depth plan
        ↓
d_mapping.json
```

### Combined Planning Model

```text
topology profile
        ↓
topology plan
        ↓
connector participation
        ↓
infrastructure role participation
        ↓
topology_signature
        ↓
infra_signature

depth profile
        ↓
depth plan
        ↓
d_mapping.json
```

### Infrastructure Role Examples

Examples of infrastructure roles:

```text
source
target
relay
primary
secondary
```

Examples of actual infrastructure:

```text
VM-A
VM-B
AWS
Azure
```

Actual infrastructure selection is outside the scope of CR-014.

### Signature Rules

- topology_signature represents the logical topology shape.
- infra_signature represents the infrastructure role participation shape.
- topology_signature is independent of suite selection.
- infra_signature is independent of suite selection.
- topology_signature is independent of execution result.
- infra_signature is independent of execution result.

### Catalog Metadata Relationship

Topology profiles may be associated with domain and solution through catalog metadata.

For PV v0.2.1:

```text
domain = backend
solution = core_runtime
```

CR-014 does not own domain selection.

CR-014 does not own solution selection.

CR-014 consumes topology definitions applicable to the selected domain and solution.

### CR-016 Relationship

CR-014 may produce topology_signature as optional execution context metadata.

When topology_signature is present in execution context, it may participate in execution_id generation under CR-016.

CR-014 does not own execution_id generation.

### CR-020 Relationship

CR-020 selects test cases through suites.

CR-014 does not select test cases.

Suite membership does not affect topology planning.

## Future Compatibility

The topology planner shall support future topology shapes without requiring redesign.

Examples include:

- migration topologies
- backup topologies
- restore topologies
- backup_restore topologies
- disaster recovery topologies
- multi-hop topologies
- fan-out topologies
- fan-in topologies
- hub-and-spoke topologies

The topology planner shall support future expansion in:

- number of logical participants
- connector roles
- infrastructure roles
- topology signatures
- infrastructure signatures
- depth profiles
- domain metadata
- solution metadata

Future topology definitions shall remain metadata-driven.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1.

A single_db topology profile produces a topology plan, topology_signature, infra_signature, and d_mapping.json.

### AC2.

A source_target topology profile produces a topology plan, topology_signature, infra_signature, and d_mapping.json.

### AC3.

Topology planning produces d_mapping.json.

### AC4.

Topology planning produces topology_signature.

### AC5.

Topology planning produces infra_signature.

### AC6.

Identical topology plans produce identical topology_signature values.

### AC7.

Identical infrastructure role participation plans produce identical infra_signature values.

### AC8.

Topology planning does not depend on suite selection.

### AC9.

Topology planning does not generate execution_id.

### AC10.

Topology planning outputs are available for execution matrix planning.

### AC11.

topology_signature can be recorded as optional execution context metadata.

### AC12.

topology_signature does not affect execution identity unless present in execution context under CR-016 rules.
