# CR-019 — Mock Connector Alignment Framework

## Target Version

v0.2.1

## Status

Draft

## Objective

Maintain architectural compatibility between Phase 1 mock-based validation and Phase 2 real-system validation. The framework ensures that mock connectors, mock topologies, and mock depth profiles remain aligned with their Phase 2 equivalents so that validation coverage developed during Phase 1 remains reusable and representative during Phase 2 execution.

## Scope

### Owns

- mock connector participation
- mock topology mapping
- mock depth profile generation
- mock topology profile generation
- mock infrastructure signature generation
- Phase 1 to Phase 2 alignment validation
- alignment traceability

### Does Not Own

- topology planning
- infrastructure selection
- execution identity generation
- functional planning
- benchmark planning
- suite selection
- report aggregation
- diagnostic analysis
- connector implementation
- real infrastructure provisioning

## Inputs

- topology profiles
- depth profiles
- connector catalogs
- mock connector definitions
- infrastructure role metadata
- alignment configuration

## Outputs

- mock topology profiles
- mock infrastructure signatures
- alignment mappings
- alignment validation results
- mock depth profiles

## Requirements

### R1.

RXTA shall support mock connector participation definitions.

### R2.

RXTA shall support mock topology profile generation.

### R3.

RXTA shall support mock depth profile generation.

### R4.

RXTA shall support mock infrastructure signature generation.

### R5.

Phase 1 architectures shall be alignable with Phase 2 architectures.

### R6.

Alignment shall be metadata-driven.

### R7.

Mock topology profiles shall be traceable to corresponding Phase 2 topology profiles.

### R8.

Mock depth profiles shall be traceable to corresponding Phase 2 depth profiles.

### R9.

Mock connector participation shall be traceable to corresponding Phase 2 connector participation.

### R10.

Mock infrastructure signatures shall be traceable to corresponding Phase 2 infrastructure role participation.

### R11.

Alignment mappings shall be deterministic for identical inputs.

### R12.

Identical alignment inputs shall produce identical alignment outputs.

### R13.

Phase 1 validation assets shall remain reusable in Phase 2.

### R14.

Alignment validation shall identify incompatible Phase 1 and Phase 2 definitions.

### R15.

Alignment validation shall identify missing Phase 2 mappings.

### R16.

Alignment validation shall identify missing Phase 1 mappings.

### R17.

Mock topology profiles shall be consumable by CR-014 topology planning.

### R18.

Mock depth profiles shall be consumable by CR-014 depth planning.

### R19.

Alignment outputs shall be consumable by CR-012 functional planning.

### R20.

Alignment outputs shall be consumable by CR-013 benchmark planning.

### R21.

Mock infrastructure signatures shall follow the infrastructure role model defined by CR-014.

### R22.

Alignment shall support current Phase 1 and Phase 2 architectures without requiring duplicate validation catalogs.

### R23.

Alignment shall remain valid when optional topology dimensions are absent.

### R24.

Alignment shall support incremental Phase 2 expansion.

### R25.

Alignment shall not require redesign when additional connector types are introduced.

## Architecture Notes

### Phase Model

```text
Phase 1
    ↓
Mock Connectors
    ↓
Mock Topologies
    ↓
Mock D Profiles

Phase 2
    ↓
Real Connectors
    ↓
Real Topologies
    ↓
Real D Profiles
```

### Alignment Model

```text
Phase 1
        ↓
Alignment Mapping
        ↓
Phase 2
```

### Connector Alignment

```text
Mock Connector
        ↓
Alignment Mapping
        ↓
Real Connector
```

Examples:

```text
Mock PostgreSQL
        ↓
PostgreSQL

Mock MySQL
        ↓
MySQL
```

### Topology Alignment

```text
Mock Topology
        ↓
Alignment Mapping
        ↓
Phase 2 Topology
```

Examples:

```text
mock_single_db
        ↓
single_db

mock_source_target
        ↓
source_target
```

### Depth Alignment

```text
Mock D Profile
        ↓
Alignment Mapping
        ↓
Phase 2 D Profile
```

### Infrastructure Signature Alignment

Mock infrastructure signatures represent infrastructure role participation only.

Examples:

```text
source
target
relay
primary
secondary
```

Mock infrastructure signatures do not represent actual infrastructure selection.

Infrastructure selection remains outside the scope of CR-019.

### Alignment Traceability

Alignment mappings shall be traceable through:

```text
phase1_artifact
phase2_artifact
alignment_status
alignment_id
```

### Relationship to CR-014

```text
CR-019
    ↓
Mock Topology Profiles
    ↓
CR-014
    ↓
Topology Planning
```

CR-019 does not perform topology planning.

CR-014 remains the owner of topology planning.

### Relationship to CR-012 and CR-013

```text
CR-019
    ↓
Alignment Outputs

CR-012
    ↓
Functional Planning

CR-013
    ↓
Benchmark Planning
```

CR-019 provides compatibility mappings.

CR-012 and CR-013 consume aligned metadata.

### Compatibility Rule

```text
Phase 1 Architecture
        ↓
Alignment
        ↓
Phase 2 Architecture
```

Validation coverage developed during Phase 1 shall remain applicable to corresponding Phase 2 architectures through alignment mappings.

## Future Compatibility

The framework shall support future alignment models without redesign.

Examples include:

- multi-hop topologies
- migration architectures
- backup and restore architectures
- disaster recovery architectures
- domain-specific connector mappings
- solution-specific connector mappings

Future connector catalogs, topology profiles, and depth profiles shall remain metadata-driven.
